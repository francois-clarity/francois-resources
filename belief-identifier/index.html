The Tree of
Clarity

One situation, taken all the way down and all the way back up. You already know the tree. This walks the red side with you first, from what you did, through what you felt and thought, to the belief underneath it. Then it turns around and builds the other side: how you would rather feel in exactly the same situation, what you would do, what your coach says, and what you choose to believe instead. You finish with one sentence to carry out with you.
