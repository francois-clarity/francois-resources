Step 1 · Your two roads

Name the roads.

Short names are fine. "Stay at the firm" and "Start the practice". "This town" and "That city". You know your two.

Step 2 · The guarantee check

What are you really shopping for?

When you say you want to make the right choice, you usually mean something more than right. Tap the ones you recognise.

None of these are for sale. No amount of research buys them. That is why the reading and the asking never settled it.

You can pick more than one. You probably will.
Step 3 · Ten years down road one

Walk ten years down the first road.

Not what you would have. Who you would be. Pick the three words that fit the person at the end of that road, then finish the sentence.

Step 4 · Ten years down road two

Now the second road.

Same walk, same question. Three words for the person at the end of this one, and the sentence.

Step 5 · The question

Two people are standing in front of you.

This is you, ten years down each road, in your own words. The question was never which road is right. It is this: which of these two people do you want to be? Tap the one.

Step 6 · One move

The road stays scary and unclear until you move on it. So make the first move small and make it this week. One call, one conversation, one hour blocked out.
