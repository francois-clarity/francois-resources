Freeing yourself from friction · the contracts you keep

Invisible
Contracts

Every relationship runs on agreements nobody wrote down. If I do my part, you should do yours. If I keep the peace, you should not upset me. If I carry the load, you should appreciate me. You may never have said it out loud. You may not even know you believe it. But the moment the other person does not deliver, you feel the breach, and you call it disrespect, being taken for granted, or betrayal. I want to walk you from that feeling back to the contract underneath it, and then help you decide what to do with it: upgrade it into a clear request, or tear it up.

Francois Esterhuizen
