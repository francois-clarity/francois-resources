The Four
Voices

Your thoughts are what turn a belief into a feeling. There are four voices doing the talking. Three of them sit on the unhealthy side and all do the same job, which is to keep the responsibility away from you. The judge aims inward and shames you. The cheerleader aims outward and blames, and it sounds like loyalty. The comforter aims inward and lets you off, and it sounds like kindness. The fourth is the coach, on the healthy side, and in anyone who feels stuck it is the underdeveloped one. This catches the first three in their own words, then builds the fourth.

Run the whole tree
