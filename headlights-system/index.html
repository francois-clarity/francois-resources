Getting Unstuck · Part Three

The Headlights System

You cannot drive with the whole journey in your headlights, and you were never supposed to. This tool takes the thing that feels like too much, parks the road you cannot see yet, and hands you your next sixty metres with a time attached.

The episode this tool comes from. It stays right here while you work.
About five minutes. Your answers stay on your device until you finish.
Step 1 · The thing

Name the thing that feels like too much.

Write it the way you would say it to a friend. "The whole business." "The house move." "My studies." Big and vague is allowed here, this is the only place it is.

Step 2 · Empty the bakkie

What is swirling inside it?

List the pieces. Every separate worry, task, and loose end that lives inside that one heavy thing. Add them one at a time, up to eight. Watching them land as separate pieces is the point.

Tap the small x on a piece to remove it.

Step 3 · The headlights

You only need sixty metres.

At night your headlights show you sixty metres of road, and that is enough to drive the whole way home. Everything you just listed is the whole journey. Tap the ONE piece that could come first. Not the biggest. The first.

Step 4 · The next visible move

Now shrink that piece to a single move you could make contact with in two minutes. Not finish. Contact. Open the document. Send the one-line email. Find the phone number.

The two-minute test: if you could not start it inside two minutes, it is still too big. Shrink it again.

Step 5 · Decide once

When are you driving?

Decide now, so starting needs no willpower later. Inside the next 24 hours works best.
