You already know what to do.

You've probably known for a while. You've read the books, made the plan, and you can explain your own patterns better than anyone around you.

And yet here you are, a year later, in the same place and doing the same thing.

Nothing's wrong with you. Your belief system has been practising to keep you safe and comfortable for years. It's sneaky and clever, and it's good at its job.

Reading about it only takes you so far, because the belief lives in your everyday choices, and that's where you have to catch it. That's what WOEMA is for: three hours of finding it in your own life, with me there to point out what you'd otherwise explain away.

Three steps, in order.

  1. 1

    Understand your own potential.

    We start with what you're capable of in your own life, and what you would be doing if nothing held you back. That picture comes first, because it's what we measure everything else against.

  2. 2

    Find what's blocking it.

    Then we look at the gap between what you know and what you actually do. There's usually a belief sitting in that gap, often an old one that made perfect sense when you learned it. We find yours and put it into plain words.

  3. 3

    Reshape your paradigm.

    By that I mean the set of beliefs you look at your life through. Once a belief is written down, you can question it and see what it has been costing you. Before we finish, you choose the first small action that starts building evidence for something different.

So you can get out of your own way.
